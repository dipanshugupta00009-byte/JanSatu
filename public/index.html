Why this exists

Three groups, one missing link

01

Citizens see it first

Communities across Jharkhand are the first to notice a broken handpump, an unsafe road crossing, or a school with no science lab — but have no structured place to file it for real evaluation.

02

Institutions have the expertise

HEIs bring research capability and a large, capable student body looking for real, community-linked problems — exactly what NEP 2020 asks for.

03

Industry brings scale

Start-ups and companies add technical depth, funding and the ability to actually deploy a working solution beyond the prototype stage.

How a challenge moves

From a photo on a phone to a resolved case file

01

Submit

02

Review

03

04

05

Resolve

Outcome logged back to the same public case file.
